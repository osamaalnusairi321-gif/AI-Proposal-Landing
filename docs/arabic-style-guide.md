# Arabic working style guide

Audience: Saudi business owners and consulting teams preparing responses to RFPs. The product creates structured first drafts from the firm's experience and documents; it does not promise finished proposals, wins, or autonomous submission.

## Voice and terminology

- Modern, approachable فصحى. Address the reader directly; use concrete verbs and short sentences. Avoid stiff nominal phrases and colloquial sales language.
- Brand: أكيورسي للاستشارات, following Accuracy's Arabic site. Product: نظام إعداد العروض. RFP: طلب تقديم عروض. First draft: مسودة أولية. Waitlist: قائمة الانتظار. Pilot: البرنامج التجريبي / شريك في البرنامج التجريبي.
- CTAs: انضم إلى قائمة الانتظار and قدّم طلبًا للمشاركة في البرنامج التجريبي. Do not use a trial CTA for the waitlist or imply instant pilot acceptance.
- Keep figures and qualifications explicit: 30 days free; 30% off the first 12 months only if subscribing; at least two RFPs, a feedback session, and relevant setup documents; approval required; documents can be redacted; no purchase obligation.
- The pilot includes the same early access, launch discount, and updates as the waitlist. Do not reintroduce the removed “No commitment” benefit.
- Form messages say what to enter and how to fix it, without blame. Required company name and website; phone optional. Email, URL and phone examples remain LTR. Explain 0–9 digits and the country code.
- Avoid English fragments in Arabic controls, alerts, accessible names, status messages or our confirmation page. Preserve the official logo artwork.

## Cadence and layout pass

Drafted sections around the user's task rather than English word order. Simplified the hero to «عروض أفضل. بجهد أقل.»; kept the RFP explanation in the following sentence. Split consent from the confidentiality/purchase note. Prefer «عند الاشتراك» to a literal conditional clause, and «مستجدات المنتج» to an English-sounding update label. Read each line as a spoken sentence during the editorial pass and remove unnecessary connective phrases.

RTL: Arabic document direction, logical spacing, mirrored decorative arrows, right-aligned labels and errors, unchanged logo. Use Accuracy's Noto Kufi Arabic / Noto Sans Arabic pairing; no Arabic italics or letter spacing. Use an explicit language switch and preserve the selected signup path and entered details when switching.
